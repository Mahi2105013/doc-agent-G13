# 📖 Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum Assistant

**Your Grounded & Faithful Companion to Islamic History**

---

## 🌟 What is it?
The **doc-agent-G13** is a state-of-the-art AI assistant designed specifically to explore the life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH). Built around the canonical texts *Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum*, *Priyonobi*, and *Sirat Ibn Hisham*, it provides accurate, grounded, and citable answers in Bengali.

## 🚀 Key Features
* **Factually Grounded:** Never guesses or hallucinates. If the answer isn't in the book, it gracefully says "I don't know."
* **Verifiable Citations:** Every claim is backed by exact paragraph citations, allowing you to instantly trace the AI's logic back to the original text.
* **Agentic Research:** If an initial search doesn't find the answer, the AI autonomously expands its search radius and re-reads the texts until it finds the truth.
* **Bengali Optimized:** Powered by specialized OCR and the advanced BAAI/bge-m3 embedding model for deep semantic understanding of Bengali queries.

## 🛡️ Built for Trust
We prioritized safety and fairness:
* **Human-in-the-Loop:** Complex or low-confidence queries are automatically flagged for human review.
* **Strict Evaluation:** Tested against rigorous splits (Train/Val/Test) at the document level to ensure zero data leakage and real-world robustness.
* **Full Audit Trail:** Every thought, tool call, and decision made by the AI is logged for complete transparency.
